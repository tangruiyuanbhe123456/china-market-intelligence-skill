# China Market Intelligence

Access Chinese market data that's invisible to Western tools.

## WeChat Index (微信指数)
- Search trends within WeChat's 1.2B users
- Compare brand awareness over time
- Available at: weixin.qq.com (Chinese only)

## Baidu Index (百度指数)
- China's Google Trends equivalent
- Search volume, demographic data, regional interest
- Critical for SEO strategy on Baidu

## 1688.com Data
- China's wholesale prices (30-50% below Alibaba.com)
- Find factories that supply Amazon bestsellers
- Check actual transaction volumes (真实成交)

## Douyin (抖音) Insights
- Trending products and categories in real-time
- Creator collaboration opportunities
- Live streaming sales data
